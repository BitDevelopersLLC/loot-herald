# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)
- First version for World of Warcraft: Forever (interface 16001).
- Role profiles for every class and talent tree (Feral is split into Bear and Cat).
- A role picker when you enter a dungeon, with an auto-suggested role.
- Upgrade, About Equal, Off-Spec and Take a Look alerts on loot rolls, with Need/Greed/Pass buttons and a roll timer.
- A verdict line on item tooltips.
- `/lh debug`, `/lh weights`, `/lh weight`, `/lh history` and `/lh test`.
- Plain-English "why" line (for example "+22 Healing, +8 Int, -5 Spirit").
- Group check: shows which party members could also use a drop.
- Bind on Equip reminder.
- Hit cap awareness (`/lh hitcap`).
- Optional group-chat line when you roll Need, saying why (`/lh announce`, off by default).
- `/lh selftest` health check for game patches.
- A settings panel.
